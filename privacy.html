Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

From Our Kitchen is a private app for sharing family recipes with people you invite. It is run by one person, Glen Bailey, not by a company, and it has no advertising, no tracking, and nothing for sale. This page explains what the app keeps, why, and how to remove it.

What the app collects

  • Your account: your email address and the name you choose to show other members. Your password goes straight to Google's Firebase sign-in service. The app itself never stores it.
  • What you add: recipes, recipe photos, comments, and photos attached to comments.
  • Your groups: which family groups you belong to, who invited you, and the join codes used.
  • Your lists: your favorites and personal collections.
  • Basic account dates: when you created your account, when you last signed in, and when you last saw the "since your last visit" notice.
  • Error reports: if the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a technical report to Sentry, an error-monitoring service. The report covers the error itself, your device model, your iOS version, and the app version. It is not tied to your name, email, or account.

The app asks for your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo, and uses only the photo you pick. It does not collect your location or contacts, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see your information

  • Members of your groups see your name, the recipes you share with their group, and your comments on those recipes.
  • Nobody else in the app can see a group's recipes. Access is controlled by the group membership rules on the server, not just by what the app shows.
  • Service providers store and process data for the app: Google Firebase for accounts, data, and photos, and Sentry for error reports. Both store this data in the United States.

Your information is never sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

What stays on your device

To work without a connection, the app keeps a copy of your recipe library and a short list of recipes you've recently viewed on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting your account removes them from that device, and so does deleting the app. Signing out does not, so a phone shared within a family keeps each person's copy separate rather than erasing it.

Emails

The app sends email only for your account: verifying your address when you sign up, and resetting your password if you ask. There is no newsletter or marketing email.

Getting a copy of your data

In the app, go to Profile → Export my data. You'll get your recipes, favorites, and collections as text you can save or send.

Deleting your account

Go to Profile → Delete account. Before anything is deleted, the app asks what should happen to each recipe you wrote, because family recipes often matter to other people too:

  • Give to someone passes the recipe to another member of the group.
  • Keep as Former Member keeps the recipe for the group, with your name replaced by "Former Member."
  • Delete removes the recipe.

Your account, profile, favorites, collections, and group memberships are then removed. A few things work differently, and you should know them in advance:

  • Comments keep your name. If you want your comments gone, delete them before you delete your account.
  • Recipes shared with a group you've already left can't be changed from the app, so they stay as they are. Email the address below and they'll be removed or anonymized for you.
  • Backups: the recipe database is backed up once a week, and each backup is deleted automatically after 60 days. Deleted information may stay in a backup until it expires.

You can also ask for your account and data to be deleted by emailing the address below.

Children

From Our Kitchen is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email the address below and it will be deleted.

Keeping it secure

All data travels over encrypted connections. Recipes, photos, and comments can be read only by members of the groups they're shared with, and only the person who wrote something can change or delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
