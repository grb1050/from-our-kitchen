Help

From Our Kitchen keeps a family's recipes in one place: the handwritten cards, the printed favorites, and the dishes people make now. It's private, so you see only the recipes shared with your groups.

Getting started

The app is invitation-only. To join, you need a join code from someone in your family who manages a group.

  1. Install From Our Kitchen from the link you were sent.
  2. Tap Create an account and enter your name, email, and a password.
  3. Open the verification email and tap the link, then return to the app and tap I've verified my email.
  4. Go to Groups → Enter a join code, type the six-character code, and confirm. The group's recipes appear within a few seconds.

Common questions

I didn't get the verification email.

Check your Junk or Spam folder first, because it often lands there. If it isn't there, tap Send it again on the verification screen. It can take a minute to arrive.

My join code doesn't work.

Each code works once and expires after 7 days. Ask the person who invited you for a new one. Letters and numbers are both accepted, and the app ignores upper and lower case.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot your password? and follow the email to set a new one.

How do I invite someone?

Open your group in Groups and tap Invite someone, then send them the code. Make a new code for each person.

How do I change my name?

Go to Profile → Edit name.

How do I leave a group?

Open the group in Groups and tap Leave this group. If you manage the group, first hand that role to another member.

Can I use it without a connection?

Yes, for reading. The recipes you've already loaded stay on your device, so you can cook without signal. Adding or changing recipes needs a connection.

How do I get a copy of my recipes?

Go to Profile → Export my data.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete account. The app explains what will happen and asks what to do with each recipe you wrote before anything is deleted. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Contact

For anything else, or to report a problem with a recipe or comment, email fromourkitchen.app@gmail.com. Please include the email address you use in the app.
